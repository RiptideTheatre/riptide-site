You'll be matched with a stranger. Neither of you will know who the other is.

For fourteen days we'll send you both a prompt each morning. Some take two minutes. Some take an afternoon. On day one you get a single message each — that's it. By the second week you'll be sharing memories you haven't told your closest friends.

There's no script, no performance, no audience. Just you, someone you've never met, and whatever happens in between.
