<p class="las__label">The Studio</p>
<h2 class="las__title">When you leave training, two things happen at once.</h2>
<div class="las__two">
<div><h3>You lose your company.</h3><p>For years you've worked every day with people who know you, push you and have your back. Then, overnight, you're on your own.</p></div>
<div><h3>Your training stops.</h3><p>Acting is a practice. If you're not doing the thing, your ability plateaus, or worse.</p></div>
</div>
<p>Leeds Actors Studio exists to fix both. It's an affordable place for recent graduates to keep training.</p>
<p>With The Company actors will join a group of actors for consistent training and rehearsals, to stay connected, stay sharp and keep growing between jobs.</p>
<p>With Uta Hagen sessions, actors can improve their technical acting with Uta Hagen's practical exercises. Ten sessions mirror Hagen's 10 exercises, giving actors a rounded, actor-focused process they can take into any rehearsal room, audition or self-tape.</p>
</div>
