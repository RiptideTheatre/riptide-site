Intermission is a pause with a structure.

You arrive through a plain door and a lift. Reception takes your coat and the world outside stops being your problem. Over eighty minutes you are guided through ten quite different sensory experiences — each one mindfully designed to explore an aspect of yourself and to make room for it.

You are alone for some of it and accompanied for the rest. You never see two of us at the same time. There is always sound.
