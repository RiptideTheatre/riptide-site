Intermission is a pause with a structure.

You arrive through a plain door and a lift. Reception takes your coat and the world outside stops being your problem. Over eighty minutes you are guided through ten quite different sensory experiences — each one mindfully designed to explore an aspect of yourself and to make room for it.

You are alone for some of it and accompanied for the rest. You never see two of us at the same time. There is always sound.

Have you ever wished that you could press pause on the fast pace of your life? Take a little time out? Maybe go to bed and watch the second half tomorrow?

What if you could take a moment to breathe in, mull over the story so far and then decide what happens next?

Well, now you can…welcome to your **Intermission**.

## This is the intersection of art, theatre and care.

Intermission is a new radical immersive experience with care at its core. Audience members will be guided on an individual journey through a series of therapeutic ‘treatments’ (scenes), each mindfully designed to reflect and reconnect with yourself.

Developed and delivered by a team of immersive theatre creators, mental health professionals, life coaches, yoga instructors, performers and masseuses. The show is grounded in a commitment to holistic health and wellness.

Innovative technology and scientific research is infused with ancient meditation and spa rituals, to produce a bespoke experience tailored to you.

## The R&D

Winter 2023, Leeds, UK.

Testing key concepts for Intermission including VR and AI application into the immersive experience, design element and developing the script.
