Sonder is the feeling of realising that everyone you pass has a life as full and complicated as your own.

This is a walk built on that idea. Binaural 360 sound puts voices at your shoulder and footsteps behind you, and GPS means the story arrives when you reach the place it belongs to. You are not following a tour. You are moving through something that is happening around you.

Passers-by will have no idea you are caught in it.
