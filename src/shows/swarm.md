SWARM was a hedonist nightclub set in the distant future, which changed depending on what the audience did throughout the night.

With multiple endings, multiple simultaneous narratives, a stock market bar and the focus on the audience to enjoy, dance and uncover the hidden narrative.

{# Earlier page copy:
Encounters in secret corners of a confetti-filled nightclub. SWARM turned Studio 24 into a working club night where actors moved among the crowd, arguments were there to be overheard, and the dance floor itself became part of the story. The audience's choices shaped how each night played out.
#}
